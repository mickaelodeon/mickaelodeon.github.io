Ground rules

These apply in every course I handle. Course-specific grading and requirements are in each course's syllabus.

01Attendance and punctuality

Be in the classroom and ready before the scheduled time. Class officially starts on the dot; after 15 minutes, the absence/late policy applies.

  • Repeated lateness may affect your participation and attendance standing.
  • University attendance policies still apply on top of this.

Legitimate circumstances will be considered appropriately.

02Quizzes and exams
  • Before: be seated and ready before the assessment begins.
  • During: follow the instructions carefully and maintain integrity.
  • Late: late students may be restricted from taking the assessment, depending on the circumstances.

Legitimate circumstances will be considered fairly and consistently.

03Technology in the classroom

No phones or personal gadgets during class unless permission is given. Devices may be used when they're:

  • required for an activity,
  • needed for programming or research,
  • used for an online assessment,
  • specifically permitted by me, or
  • needed for legitimate academic or accessibility purposes.

Technology is a learning tool, not a distraction.

04Respect the classroom
  • Be ready — seated and prepared when class begins.
  • Be present — give your attention to the discussion.
  • Be responsible — clean your area and take care of classroom resources.
  • Be respectful — don't interrupt, ridicule, or distract others.

If I arrive and the class is excessively noisy, people are standing around, or the room is clearly unprepared, I may conduct a short on-the-spot assessment. Instructional time is valuable, and I expect everyone to come ready to learn.

05Your work must be your work
  • No cheating during quizzes and examinations.
  • No plagiarism — give credit where credit is due.
  • No copying — don't submit another student's work.
  • No misrepresentation — don't claim work you didn't actually perform.

When in doubt, ask before submitting.

06Submissions
  • Submit through the designated platform.
  • Follow the required format and the correct filename.
  • Check your file before submitting.
  • Submit before the deadline.

"I submitted the wrong/corrupted file" is still a submission problem. Double-check your work — accountability matters in professional environments.

07Group work is not a free ride

Every member is accountable. Participate and communicate, meet deadlines, contribute meaningfully, document your contribution, and tell me early when problems come up.

Being in a group does not automatically mean receiving the same individual performance grade.

08How we communicate

Announcements go through the class group or LMS. Submissions go through the LMS or designated platform. Academic questions belong in class or during consultation. Personal concerns go through private message or email.

Please communicate respectfully and professionally, and allow reasonable response time.
